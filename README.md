# HeroDangle

**App coming soon as a downloadable .exe!**

Built HeroDangle, a personal-use Windows desktop app inspired by the macOS "Lucky Dangle" — a physics-based charm that hangs on a thread from the top of your screen, ready whenever you want it.

Press a hotkey, and the charm drops down onto your desktop, swinging and responding naturally with rope-like physics. Press it again to tuck it away. It sits over your other windows without getting in the way of your clicks.

## Features

- **Instant summon** - press a global hotkey from anywhere to bring the charm down onto your screen
- **Realistic physics** - a verlet-rope simulation makes the charm swing, bounce, and respond naturally to movement
- **Click-through overlay** - the charm floats over your desktop and other apps without blocking clicks underneath
- **Lightweight & always ready** - runs quietly in the background until you call it

## How to Use

1. **Launch HeroDangle** - run the app once downloaded
2. **Press the hotkey** - your charm drops down from the top of the screen
3. **Play with it** - flick or move your cursor near it to see the physics respond
4. **Press again to dismiss** - tuck the charm away whenever you don't need it
